Your job search, as two agent skills.

Find roles you can actually get, then apply through your own Chrome — with a local tracker that remembers everything.

install — one line 
$ npx skills add xpressabhi/job-search-skills \
    -s job-finder -s apply-to-jobs -a '*' -g -y

What you get

Two skills that work together: one finds and remembers, one applies — both on your machine.

01

job-finder

Interviews you once from your CV, then sweeps your companies and boards every run — filtering by real eligibility, not marketing.

  • Remote region, timezone, and comp floor enforced
  • Learns companies you like, ignores the ones you don't
  • Never surfaces the same role twice
02

apply-to-jobs

Autofills and submits applications in your own visible Chrome — the form sees you, not a bot service.

  • Ashby · Greenhouse · Lever · Workable · Workday +3
  • Pauses only on captchas, logins, consent, knockouts
  • Answers banked so nothing is ever invented

How it works

One setup conversation. After that it's one-liners.

step 1

Onboard once

Point it at your CV. It confirms what it found and asks only what the CV can't answer.

step 2

Sweep & rank

Starter companies, learned sources, and boards — verified live, ranked by fit.

step 3

Apply

Say “apply to all interested”. Chrome does the typing; the tracker records every outcome.

Local by default

No server, no account, no external apply service. Profile, history, notes, and CV stay on disk.
